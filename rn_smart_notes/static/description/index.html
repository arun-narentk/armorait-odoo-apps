Smart Notes

Context-aware sticky notes on any business document

Attach colorful sticky notes to sale orders, invoices, CRM leads, contacts, products, and employees. Pin important reminders, mention teammates, and keep critical customer context visible without opening the chatter.

Business Problem

Payment warnings, VIP flags, and delivery instructions are often buried in chatter, spreadsheets, or personal notebooks. Teams miss context because it is not visible on the document they are working on.

Business Solution

Smart Notes adds a fast annotation layer to supported Odoo records. Users see pinned cards directly on the form, add quick templates in one click, mention teammates, and schedule reminders that create activities automatically.

  • Notes panel on sale orders, purchases, invoices, CRM, contacts, products, employees
  • Smart button counter opens full kanban and list views
  • Color-coded cards with icons and priorities
  • Private, sales team, manager, and everyone visibility
  • Search notes by keyword across the company
