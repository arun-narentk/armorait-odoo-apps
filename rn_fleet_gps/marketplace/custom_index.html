Overview

Forecast, stock visibility, and inventory ops in Odoo

Extends standard Fleet with a provider-agnostic GPS layer.

Fleet GPS Tracking driver assignment, fuel and expense logs, geofences, alerts, It is built for Odoo 19 Community by ARMORA IT Technologies.

The Problem

  • Stock decisions rely on ad-hoc Excel forecasts
  • Replenishment signals arrive too late
  • Hard to jump from a product to related moves
  • Multi-warehouse visibility is weak
  • Alerts for stockouts are scattered

The Solution

  • Inventory foresight inside Odoo
  • Smart buttons to related stock documents
  • Dashboards for open and risk items
  • Warehouse-aware operations
  • Clear access for inventory roles

Installation

  1. Add rn_fleet_gps to your addons path
  2. Restart the Odoo service
  3. Apps → Update Apps List
  4. Search Fleet GPS Tracking
  5. Click Install
  6. Complete settings and use the Moves smart workflows
