# Profit Guard

        Margin protection and dead stock intelligence

        ## Business problem
        Sales teams confirm low-margin orders while dead stock locks working capital silently.

        ## Solution
        Profit Guard blocks or warns on low-margin order lines and highlights slow and dead inventory with clearance suggestions.

        ## Key benefits
        - Protect gross margin
- Surface dead stock value
- Manager override audit
- Inventory aging insight
- Configurable thresholds
