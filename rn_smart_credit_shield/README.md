# Credit Control

        Credit limits, risk scoring, and payment reminders

        ## Business problem
        Sales confirms orders for customers who are already overdue or over credit limit.

        ## Solution
        Credit Control scores partner risk, enforces limits, blocks risky orders, and automates WhatsApp payment reminders.

        ## Key benefits
        - Lower bad debt
- Automated risk scoring
- Order blocks with override
- Exposure dashboards
- WhatsApp payment nudges
