## 3. ERP IMPLEMENTATION PAGE

**H1:** ERP Implementation Services

**What is ERP Implementation**  
ERP implementation is the process of deploying one integrated system (here, Odoo) to run core operations: finance, inventory, sales, purchasing and—as needed—manufacturing and HR. It replaces spreadsheets and separate tools with a single source of truth.

**Our Implementation Approach (5 steps)**  
1. **Discovery & requirements** — Map processes, pain points and goals. Define scope and success criteria.  
2. **Design & configuration** — Configure Odoo to your workflows, chart of accounts, and reporting needs.  
3. **Build & migrate** — Customize where needed; migrate data from existing systems with validation.  
4. **Test & train** — UAT with your team; training so users can operate and maintain the system.  
5. **Go-live & support** — Cutover, hypercare and handover to ongoing support.

**Industries We Support**  
Manufacturing, trading and distribution, retail, and service companies. We adapt the same approach to your industry and scale.

**Benefits of ERP**  
- One system for orders, inventory, finance and reporting.  
- Fewer manual entries and reconciliations.  
- Faster month-end and accurate stock and costing.  
- Clear audit trail and controls.

**Why Choose Armorait**  
Structured methodology, Odoo expertise, and a single team for implementation and support. We work to fixed scope and timelines where possible.

## 4. ODOO CUSTOMIZATION PAGE

**H1:** Odoo ERP Customization Services

**Overview of Odoo ERP**  
Odoo is a modular ERP: you use the apps you need (sales, inventory, accounting, manufacturing, HR, etc.) and extend them with custom logic, fields and integrations. Armorait builds those extensions so Odoo fits your processes.

**Customization Capabilities**  
- Custom fields, views and dashboards.  
- Workflow and approval rules.  
- Reports and documents (quotes, delivery, invoices).  
- Industry-specific logic (e.g. batch, BOM, project billing).

**Module Expertise**  
- **Manufacturing:** BOM, work orders, scheduling, costing.  
- **Accounting:** Chart of accounts, multi-currency, reconciliation, reporting.  
- **HR:** Employees, leave, attendance, payroll integration.  
- **CRM:** Pipeline, activities, lead/opportunity management.  
- **Inventory:** Multi-warehouse, lots, routes, reordering rules.

**Integration Services**  
REST/API and connector-based integrations with e-commerce, payment gateways, shipping, and other business apps. We design for security and maintainability.

**Support & Maintenance**  
Post-launch support, Odoo upgrades, and training. Custom code kept compatible with standard Odoo updates.
