AI Copilot for Odoo

A self-service business assistant for Sales, Accounting, Inventory, and CRM. Install it in your own Odoo, configure it, and use it with your own data.

  • Suggested questions on every new chat
  • Built-in analytics tools for daily manager questions
  • Action cards: Open List and Create Activity
  • Permission-aware ORM tools, no generated SQL

Use It On Your Own

This module is designed for self-use by your own team. You install, configure, and operate it in your own Odoo environment.

Quick Start

  1. Install AI Copilot for Odoo from Apps.
  2. Open AI Copilot > Settings and enable it.
  3. Open AI Copilot > Ask Copilot.
  4. Pick a suggested question or type your own.
  5. Use action cards to open related records and follow up.

Best for operations teams who want practical answers from Odoo data without custom development.
