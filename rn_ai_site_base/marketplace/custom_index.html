Overview

An AI Business Launch Platform, not just a page generator

Most teams still describe the business in chat, then rebuild the website by hand in Odoo. That slows launches and leaves SEO, structure, and messaging inconsistent.

AI Business Launch Core captures a business brief (type, location, tone, audience, keywords), generates a multi-page website project with content blocks and theme presets, stores SEO metadata per page, and prepares publish workflow hooks for Odoo Website and companion modules.

The Problem

  • Business ideas stay in notes and chat threads
  • Website pages are built page-by-page by hand
  • SEO titles and descriptions are often missing
  • Theme and messaging drift across pages
  • No clear path from brief to publish in Odoo

The Solution

  • Capture a structured Business Brief
  • Generate pages, blocks, and theme in one flow
  • SEO metadata and OG fields per page
  • Operations dashboard for project status
  • Publish hooks ready for Odoo Website sync

Installation

  1. Purchase and download the module ZIP from the Odoo Apps Store
  2. Extract rn_ai_site_base into your custom addons path
  3. Restart Odoo and update the Apps list
  4. Install AI Business Launch Core
  5. Create a Business Brief and generate a website project
  6. Assign security groups and open the operations dashboard
